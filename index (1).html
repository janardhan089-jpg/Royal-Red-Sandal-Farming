Before you decide

Independent verification matters

Promotional statements, certificates, return estimates, and legal claims should be checked against original records.

Documents to request

  • ✓Current title deed and encumbrance certificate
  • ✓Survey map, boundaries, access rights, and land classification
  • ✓Draft sale/lease agreement and complete fee schedule
  • ✓Written explanation of tree ownership and harvest/sale permissions

Keep expectations realistic

Farmland and tree-plantation projects can involve land-title, regulatory, environmental, liquidity, weather, disease, market-price, and management risks. Do not rely on projected returns alone. Verify every claim independently and consult a qualified lawyer and financial adviser before committing money.
