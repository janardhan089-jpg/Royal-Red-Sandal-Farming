About Red Sandalwood

Red sandalwood, scientifically known as Pterocarpus santalinus, is a tree species native to parts of southern India and especially associated with the Eastern Ghats of Andhra Pradesh.

What to know

  • Site conditions and drainage influence plantation performance.
  • Tree growth and heartwood development vary with location and management.
  • Plant survival, maintenance, protection and time horizon matter.
  • Commercial value depends on multiple factors; future prices are uncertain.

Before planting

  • Assess soil, water, climate and land suitability locally.
  • Buy healthy, traceable planting material.
  • Confirm land use and applicable forestry requirements.
  • Keep planting, maintenance and tree-survival records.

For technical guidance, consult forestry experts and current official publications. This page is general information, not a substitute for local professional advice.

Legal & Due-Diligence Guide

Land ownership and red sandalwood cultivation, felling, transport and export may be subject to different rules. Requirements can depend on the state, land status, species and intended activity.

Land checks

  • Current title and ownership records
  • Encumbrance certificate and relevant revenue records
  • Survey numbers, boundaries and legal access
  • Land-use status and applicable development approvals
  • Written terms for sale, lease, maintenance and other charges

Tree and trade checks

  • Ask the relevant Andhra Pradesh Forest Department office about current rules.
  • Confirm requirements before felling or transporting red sandalwood.
  • Do not assume export is permitted without the required authorisation.
  • Obtain independent legal advice before signing or paying.

Official resources: Andhra Pradesh Forest Department · Directorate General of Foreign Trade · Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change.

This is a general information checklist, not legal advice or confirmation that a specific project is compliant. Requirements should be verified with the relevant authorities and a qualified lawyer.
